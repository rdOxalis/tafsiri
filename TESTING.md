# Become a tafsiri Tester

tafsiri is an AI-powered translation app for language learners. It's currently in closed beta on Google Play, and we'd love your help testing it!

## How to Join

1. **Join the tester group**
   Join the `tafsiri-tester` Google Group:
   👉 https://groups.google.com/g/tafsiri-tester

   You'll need a Google account to join. Membership is open — anyone is welcome.

2. **Opt in to the closed test**
   Once you're a group member, open the tafsiri opt-in link on the Android device you want to test with:
   👉 https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.njerahouse.tafsiri

   Make sure you're signed into the **same Google account** on your device that you used to join the group.

3. **Install tafsiri**
   After opting in, the link will take you to the tafsiri page on Google Play. Install the app as usual.

## Giving Feedback

- Post in the [tafsiri-tester group](https://groups.google.com/g/tafsiri-tester) to share feedback, ask questions, or report bugs.
- Alternatively, open an [issue on GitHub](../../issues) if you're comfortable with GitHub.

## Notes

- You can leave the Google Group at any time — this will also remove your access to the closed test.
- Please actually try the app after installing! Active usage helps us a lot during testing.

Thanks for helping make tafsiri better! 🙌
