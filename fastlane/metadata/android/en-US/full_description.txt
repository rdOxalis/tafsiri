Tafsiri is a translation app for people learning a language. "Tafsiri" is Swahili for "translation".

It translates with the AI provider you choose — Mistral, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI ChatGPT — using your own API key. There is no account, no subscription and no server in between: the app talks to the provider directly, and you pay only for what you use. Mistral's free tier is enough to try it out.

How it works
You set a learning language and a language you are confident in. Text in your learning language is translated into your confident language; anything else is translated into your learning language. There is no direction to pick — the app works it out from each input.

For learners
* Correction mode: write in the language you are learning and get it back corrected, with a note on every change. Words you did not know and wrote in another language are replaced with the right ones.
* Word explanations: the essential words of a translation, with part of speech and meaning, written in your confident language.
* Choose the model for each provider — a cheaper one, or the most accurate.

Input
* Type or paste.
* Speak, using your device's speech recognition.
* Read text from photos and screenshots with Tesseract, on the device, Cyrillic included. The recognition data for your two languages is downloaded once from the Tesseract project; no image ever leaves the device.

Also
* History with favourites; tap an entry to load it again.
* Backup and restore of settings and history to a file you choose.
* Interface in 12 languages: English, German, Swahili, French, Dutch, Spanish, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Polish, Italian and Bulgarian.

Privacy
Your API keys stay on your device and go only to the provider you selected, and your text is sent only to that provider. No analytics, no tracking — the app collects nothing.
