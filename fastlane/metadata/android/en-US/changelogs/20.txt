Tafsiri was dropping grammatical number when a language marks it on the verb rather than the noun: "Salim gave the cat food" when he fed several. Found by a tester, settled by measuring models against each other rather than guessing.

Claude now uses Sonnet 5.5 by default. It gets this right and costs about twice as much per request, so Settings now lets you choose the model for each provider — including the cheaper one.
