Reading text from images now uses Tesseract, an open-source engine built into the app, instead of Google ML Kit. Tafsiri contains no Google code any more.

New: Cyrillic and other scripts can be read. An unclear photo is refused instead of guessed at.

Slower: recognition takes a little longer, and the first image downloads the data for your two languages once (a few MB). Angled or dark photos are read less reliably.

The app is about 10 MB smaller.
